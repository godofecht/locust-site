Why Locust exists

Connectivity should reduce burden, not export it.

Local delivery is valuable. Its current economics can shift vehicle costs, working-time risk and weak task-price visibility onto couriers. UK public bodies document these issues; Locust exists to test a logistics model with explicit labour and public-impact accounting.910

Our position Automate the repetitive movement. Keep accountable people in the system.

Autonomy should reduce avoidable driving and precarious tasks while creating skilled work in operations, maintenance, infrastructure, customer support and safety.

Where the idea began

The convenience economy has costs its checkout screens rarely show.

Food and parcel couriers can face unpredictable task pricing, personal vehicle costs, time pressure and limited visibility into platform decisions. Migrants and graduates are not the problem; vulnerability and weak bargaining power are. A better logistics model should improve service without depending on desperation.9

01

Externalised road cost

Small urgent orders can trigger a full vehicle journey, adding traffic, fuel exposure, parking pressure and collision risk.

02

Opaque labour incentives

Official UK research records variable task rates, unpaid waiting and worker-borne costs. Automation must not reproduce that opacity.9

03

One-way connectivity

Most platforms optimise retailer-to-consumer flow. A genuinely connected city should also support bounded person-to-person and community logistics.

Why now

The technology is entering a useful, not magical, phase.

Operators now publish useful payload, service-time and cumulative-volume references. Those figures establish technical plausibility, not local economics or public acceptance. No aircraft is silent; every route still needs measured acoustic evidence.456

OperationsOperators now report real volume

Wing, Manna and Walmart publish cumulative deliveries, service times and payload limits that can seed a model before local data exists.456

EconomicsCapital and energy still matter

Bank Rate was 3.75% on 6 June 2026. The case for simulation-first deployment is disciplined capital allocation, not a claim that financing is uniquely expensive.11

LimitsNot every basket belongs in the air

Distance, mass, liquids, weather, noise, risk and handoff quality decide whether a drone, cargo bike, van or collection point is the right mode.

The cooperative compact

Different capital should buy different outcomes.

Locust sits between public value, aerospace R&D and commercial return. The programme works only when those objectives are separated, measured and coordinated.

Council

Public value without aircraft-development risk

Contribute sites, data, coordination and evaluation. Gain local evidence, resilience options, transparent safeguards, access improvements and authority over public assets and operating windows.

Government

Reusable UK capability

Fund industrial research, assurance methods, HIL and transferable civil-RPAS technology rather than subsidising a single local delivery service. ATI explicitly assesses technology, market, exploitation and UK benefit.3

Investors

Compounding commercial assets

Fund the team, IP, integrations, working capital and customer acquisition. Gain exposure to project revenue, recurring software and an evidence base that improves with each deployment.

Customers

Evidence before commitment

Buy a route and mission study first. Commit to aircraft and infrastructure only when service, safety and economics pass agreed gates.

Communities

Visibility and recourse

Receive published operating limits, privacy rules, complaint routes, performance reporting and a voice at route and expansion gates.

Locust

Accountable integration

Own the digital twin, partner interfaces, evidence workflow and commercial product while the authorised operator retains operational safety authority.

Rules we will operate by

Transformative only counts when it remains accountable.

  1. 01No unlimited pilotBound geography, hours, payloads, weather and stop conditions.
  2. 02No hidden labour caseShow supervisors, mechanics, recovery teams and customer support in the economics.
  3. 03No “silent” claimMeasure sound by aircraft, route, altitude, time and receptor.
  4. 04No mode ideologyUse drones only where they outperform the practical ground alternative.
  5. 05No evidence theatreLabel simulation, assumptions, physical measurements and approvals separately.
